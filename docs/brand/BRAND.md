# Brand

**Verdict: know you're ready.** The brand promise is certainty: Verdict tells students the truth about their readiness, shows the evidence, and helps them fix the gaps.

## Personality
The best teaching assistant you ever had: fair, clear, calm, rigorous and warm. Never harsh, vague, hyped, pedantic or cutesy. Archetype: the Sage, warmed by the Caregiver.

## Voice
Say the true thing, kindly, in plain words, and end with the next step. Full rules and examples: `.claude/skills/verdict-voice/SKILL.md`.

## Logo
- Wordmark **verdict.** in Readex Pro SemiBold; the full stop is the only colour (green). Files in `logo/`: `verdict-wordmark.svg` for light backgrounds, `verdict-wordmark-on-dark.svg` for dark backgrounds (both transparent), and `verdict-wordmark-dark.svg` on a solid ink background.
- App icon **v.** on ink.
- README banner: `banner.svg`, the wordmark and tagline on the Space background, with the text drawn as outlines.
- Never recolour, move or remove the full stop; never stretch, outline or add effects; never retype the name in another font. Minimum width 72px; below that use the icon.
- The ready badge (a tick starting from a dot) marks mastered topics in the product; it is not a logo.

## Visual direction: the Globe
The product is dark-first: a calm space background (drifting nebula, twinkling stars, a rare shooting star), solid panels for content, and glowing green for progress and the main action. The student's course is a 3D globe of lights: green mastered, amber in progress, dark not started. Verdict sentences end with a coloured full stop ("Almost ready." in amber). Tokens: `tokens.json`. Rules: `docs/UX-GUIDELINES.md`.

## Typography
Readex Pro for Latin and Arabic (it extends Lexend, designed around research on reading fluency). Readex Pro and Noto Sans Arabic are licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/); the wordmark is drawn from Readex Pro's outlines, which the licence allows. Regular 400 for reading, SemiBold 600 and Bold 700 for headings and buttons. Body 15 to 17px on phones; never below 13px for anything a student must read to act.
