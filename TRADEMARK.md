# Trademark policy

"Verdict" and the Verdict logos (the **verdict.** wordmark and the **v.** app icon) are trademarks of Rayan Alyasi. The code is open source under the [AGPL](LICENSE); the trademarks are not. This policy lets everyone talk about and build on Verdict, while making sure nobody is misled about who makes what.

## You may, without asking
- **Refer to Verdict** truthfully, in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, comparisons and course materials.
- **Describe compatibility,** for example "works with Verdict" or "based on Verdict", as long as it's clear your project isn't Verdict.
- **Run an unmodified copy** for yourself or your organisation's own members, such as a university's students, and call it Verdict.
- **Use the logo to link** to the project or this repository.

## Ask first
Open an issue, or email rayanalyasi@gmail.com, before you:
- offer Verdict to the public or to customers as a hosted service under the Verdict name;
- use the name or logo in a product, company or domain name;
- make merchandise.

## Not allowed
- **Using the name or logo for a modified version.** Forks need their own name and logo. You may say they're "based on Verdict".
- **Implying endorsement** or an official connection that doesn't exist.
- **Altering the logo:** recolouring, distorting or combining it with other marks.
- **Any use that misleads people** or harms the project's reputation.
