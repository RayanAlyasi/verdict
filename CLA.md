## How to sign
Sign once, from the GitHub account you contribute with, by commenting exactly this on your pull request:

> I have read the Verdict CLA and I agree to its terms.

Your comment is your acceptance of this Agreement, with the same effect as a written signature. The project records your GitHub username and numeric account ID, the pull request, a link to your comment and the time. These records are public, on this repository's `cla-signatures` branch.
