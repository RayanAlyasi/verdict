# Verdict voice

Write like the best teaching assistant a student ever had: say the true thing, kindly, in plain words, and end with the next step.

## Rules
1. Name the gap and pair it with the fix.
2. Use plain words and short sentences a first-year in any major would say.
3. Prefer numbers and sources to praise; never "amazing" or "10x".
4. End with one next step.
5. Explain every number or visual in words.
6. Use sentence case; no exclamation marks in system messages; no emoji in the interface.
7. Write Arabic in clear, friendly Modern Standard Arabic, not formal and not heavy dialect.

## Examples
| Moment | Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong answer | "Wrong! Try again 😅" | "Not quite. You converted the units after the formula. Look at step 2." |
| Stuck | "Here's the answer." | "Check the units in step 2. What changes?" |
| Progress | "You're doing great!!" | "9 of 12 topics mastered. Next: enzyme kinetics, about 25 minutes." |
| Error | "Something went wrong." | "We couldn't read page 4 of this file. Try a clearer photo or upload the PDF." |

## Gotchas
- A bare percentage confuses students; say what it measures ("of what's on your midterm").
- "Ready / almost / not yet" counts need their noun ("7 of 12 midterm topics mastered").
- During practice, hints must never contain the final value or the correct option.
